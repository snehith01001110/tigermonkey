    cabo

    Have the lowest total when the round ends. Remember your hidden cards and replace high ones with low ones.

    How a turn works

    1. Remember your hand. You have four face-down cards. At the start, you may look at only your bottom two.
    2. Choose a card. Draw from the deck or take the top discard.
    3. Keep it or discard it. Replace one of your cards, or discard a deck card to use its power.

    Power cards

    A power works only when you draw that card from the deck and discard it.

    7–8look at one of your cards

    Jswap one of yours with one of theirs, without looking

    Match the discard

    At the start of your turn, you can remove one of your cards if it has the same rank as the top discard. Get it right and the space stays empty; get it wrong and you take a penalty card.

    End the round

    Call Cabo when you think your score is lowest. Your opponent gets one final turn, then both hands are revealed and scored. Jokers are 0 and red kings are −1.

    Empty your hand quickly, or call Yaniv when your cards total 5 or less. Lowest match score wins.

    On your turn

    1. Play one card.
    2. Take a matching card from the table if there is one.
    3. Otherwise, take table cards that add up to your card.

    If nothing matches, your card stays on the table. Clear the table to score a scopa.
